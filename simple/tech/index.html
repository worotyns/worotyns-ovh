Research & development

WDFT is a fintech R&D company I co-own and drive. We publish concepts as open standards, prototype them, then implement them as products.

  • Federated Payment Standard — an open payment protocol built on banking infrastructure instead of card networks: a federated registry of banks and providers, SDK-generated links and QR codes that open prefilled in the payer's banking app, and two signed webhook confirmations — one when the user approves, one when the funds actually settle. The registry never touches money. Spec plus a sandbox for bank partners. Whitepaper
  • Session-Based Payments — short-lived usage sessions: a user makes small commitments across partner sites, the operator aggregates them into a single settlement and then reports and pays out. Four roles — user, operator, partner, bank — and a settlement step that has to stay idempotent. Runs in production as GetViaMsg. Whitepaper
