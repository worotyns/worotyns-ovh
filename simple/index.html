Business Technology

I help founders and product teams decide what to build, what to charge for it, and how to sell it. Ten years at PushPushGo — the company I co-founded — took it from a first prototype to two hundred enterprise clients and a majority acquisition by WSE-listed Vercom S.A. in 2021. I ran the product organisation: roadmap, pricing, packaging, positioning, hiring.

Today I do the same work for other companies, and I still operate two products of my own — which is why the advice is grounded in shipping and invoices, not in slides. If your problem is on the engineering side, there is a technology track.

What I help with

  • Product strategy and roadmap — what to build, what to kill, in what order, prioritised against revenue rather than opinions.
  • Pricing and monetisation — tier design, the value metric behind it, commission and freemium models, margin per segment, rollout without churning the existing base.
  • Go-to-market and positioning — who it is for, what it replaces, the sentence that makes it obvious, channels that fit the price point.
  • Fractional CPO — running the product organisation: process, cadence, discovery, and hiring or mentoring the first PM.

Track record

From a side project to the leading push-notification platform in Poland: two hundred enterprise clients including TUI, POLITICO, Auchan, Hearst and Interia, a team of fifteen, and a majority acquisition by Vercom S.A. in 2021. Co-founder, then CPO, then CTO.

GetViaMsg — getviamsg.wdft.ovh

57% of readers leave when they hit a paywall. GetViaMsg gives publishers a pay-as-you-go option: one SMS unlocks one article, no card and no account, commission instead of fixed fees, and payout the next business day instead of after sixty. Beta, going to market. Co-founder and CTO.

AI, in practice

Most teams buy AI and then wonder where the value went. I help pick the two or three workflows where it pays for itself — and where it is a toy — with cost per outcome rather than cost per token as the measure, privacy rules about what may leave the building, and conventions and review so the output stays trustworthy. Two of my own products are AI products: Krazeta and uff.email.

Research & development

WDFT is a fintech R&D company I co-own and drive. We publish concepts as open standards, prototype them, then implement them as products.

  • Federated Payment Standard — an open payment protocol over existing banking rails instead of card networks, for under 0.01 PLN per transaction, with no intermediaries and no chargebacks. That makes per-article and per-cent pricing possible in the first place. Spec plus a sandbox for bank partners. Whitepaper
  • Session-Based Payments — instead of one subscription or nothing, the reader commits a few groszy per article inside a session and settles it with a single transfer later. The concept I took from whitepaper to live product as GetViaMsg. Whitepaper
