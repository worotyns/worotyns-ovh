01 Concept · spec + PoC sandbox Open standard · in research

Federated Payment Standard

An open payment protocol built on banking infrastructure instead of card networks: a federated registry of banks and providers, SDK-generated links and QR codes that open prefilled in the payer's banking app, and two signed webhook confirmations — one when the user approves, one when the funds actually settle. The registry never touches money.

Card networks make small payments uneconomical — roughly 0.25 PLN plus 1–3% per transaction. This standard moves money directly from the buyer's bank to the merchant's bank over rails that already exist, for under a grosz per transaction. Without chargebacks, reserves or gateway contracts, per-article and per-cent pricing becomes possible in the first place.

  • Federated bank registry
  • Signed webhooks
  • Bank SDK & payment links
  • < 0.01 PLN per transaction
  • PoC sandbox
  • Open standard
  • < 0.01 PLN per transaction
  • No intermediaries
  • No chargebacks or reserves
  • Bank-ready spec

02 Concept · shipped as GetViaMsg Concept · turned into a product

Session-Based Payments

Short-lived usage sessions instead of one-off transactions: the user makes small commitments across partner sites, the operator aggregates them into a single settlement, then reports and pays out to each partner. Four roles — user, operator, partner, bank — and a settlement step that has to stay idempotent.

Instead of one subscription or nothing, a reader commits a few groszy per article inside a session and settles it with a single transfer later. That opens up the readers who would never subscribe — the concept I took from whitepaper to live product as GetViaMsg.

  • Session & commitment model
  • Aggregated settlement
  • Operator / partner / bank roles
  • Payouts & reporting
  • Runs in production
  • Pay-as-you-go
  • One settlement per session
  • Monetises non-subscribers
