Research & development — WDFT, co-owned

Business

Federated Payment Standard

Card fees make small payments uneconomical: roughly 0.25 PLN plus 1–3% per transaction. This standard moves money between banks over rails that already exist, for under a cent — so per-article and per-cent pricing becomes possible at all.

  • No intermediaries, no chargebacks, no reserves.
  • Aimed at banks and payment providers.

wdft.ovh

Technology

Federated Payment Standard

An open payment protocol over existing banking infrastructure instead of card networks: a federated registry of banks and providers, SDK-generated links and QR codes that open prefilled in the payer's banking app, and two signed webhook confirmations — one when the user approves, one when funds settle. The registry never handles money, payment links are signature-verified and keys rotate.

status · spec + PoC sandbox for banks

Session-Based Payments

Instead of one subscription or nothing, a reader commits a few cents per article inside a session and settles it with a single transfer later — which monetises the readers who would never subscribe.
