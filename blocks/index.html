Federated Payment Standard

An open payment protocol over existing banking rails instead of card networks: a federated registry of banks, SDK payment links and QR codes that open prefilled in the payer's banking app, two signed confirmations. Under 0.01 PLN per transaction, no intermediaries, no chargebacks.

Session-Based Payments

Short-lived sessions with small commitments, aggregated into a single settlement and paid out to partners. Four roles — user, operator, partner, bank — and a settlement step that has to stay idempotent.
